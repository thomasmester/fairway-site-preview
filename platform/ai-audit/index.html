Fairway’s AI audit reviews a title and registration packet — every field, form and signature, including handwritten documents — against state and county rules, and returns an instant pass or fail with the reason for any failure. Errors are fixed before the packet reaches the DMV, so rejections and rework drop.

What it does

Instantly check every field, form and signature

  • Completeness. Every form the transaction requires is present.
  • Field accuracy. Every field is filled in correctly and consistently across documents, such as VINs and sales prices.
  • Signatures and handwriting. Handwritten documents and signatures are read and checked.
  • Rule compliance. Documents are validated against the current requirements of the state and county.
  • Clear outcomes. A pass or fail decision, with the reason for each failure, so the fix is obvious.
How it works

How the audit works

  1. Upload

    Upload the packet as a PDF. It doesn’t need to be sorted.

  2. Audit

    AI checks it against the rules engine in real time. Texas deal jackets are checked in about two minutes.

  3. Resolve

    Correct the document or mark a flag incorrect. Fairway can message customers to request fixes.

  4. Submit

    Send a complete, compliant packet to the DMV.

FAQ

AI Paperwork Audit: frequently asked questions

Is the audit really instant?

Yes. Fairway processes paperwork, including in-state and out-of-state transfers and renewals, in real time. It checks that every required form is included and every field is filled correctly, and returns a pass or fail decision with the reasons for any failure.

How accurate is the AI audit?

Fairway’s automated auditing outperforms manual review on error rates, finding twice as many errors as manual review.

Can it read handwritten documents?

Yes. Fairway checks every field, form and signature, including handwritten documents.

What happens when a document fails?

You get the reason for each failure. Staff correct the document or mark the flag incorrect, and Fairway can automatically message customers to request fixes.

Is an AI deal jacket audit the same thing?

Yes. For dealers and lenders, the deal jacket is the packet being audited; Fairway checks it the same way against the rules of the titling state and county.
