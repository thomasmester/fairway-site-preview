Privacy policy

Last updated February 26, 2026

This Privacy Notice for Palermo Solutions, Inc. (doing business as “Fairway”) (“we,” “us,” or “our”) describes how and why we may access, collect, store, use, and/or share (“process”) your personal information when you use our services (“Services”), including when you:

  • Visit our website at https://www.getfairway.com or any website of ours that links to this Privacy Notice.
  • Use Fairway Title and Registration Automation. Fairway provides an integrated suite of AI-powered tools that automate every step of the title and registration lifecycle. Its AI Title Clerk technology audits title applications for completeness and accuracy — verifying data across handwritten and digital documents, auto-filling required forms, and flagging discrepancies before submission. The system enforces authoritative DMV rules at the state and county level, resulting in faster approvals, fewer rejections, and reduced administrative cost. Across all products, Fairway’s architecture is API-first, enabling seamless white-label integration for partners and agencies. By combining automated rule enforcement, intelligent document verification, and embedded distribution, Fairway is establishing the statewide compliance standard in multiple jurisdictions — processing millions of audited transactions annually and advancing its mission to make vehicle titling and registration effortless, accurate, and trusted.

If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at info@getfairway.com.
